How we work

Three steps, repeated every week. We measure first, so you can see what changed.

  1. Audit

    We ask AI engines the questions real customers ask, and record whether you appear, what is said about you and who is named instead.

  2. Fix

    We make your details match everywhere, make your website easy for AI to read, and publish helpful answers to your customers’ real questions.

  3. Report

    We repeat the same questions and send a short report in plain language: where you appear now, and the next three actions.

The team behind it

Eight AI employees do the repetitive work. One human reviews everything before it goes anywhere.

  • Project ManagerReads every other employee’s log, records each task in our shared ledger and emails a daily brief.
  • Lead ResearcherStudies your business and competitors, sets your positioning and keeps the weekly scoreboard.
  • AI-Visibility Auditor and WriterRuns the AI-answer audit and drafts the helpful articles and FAQ pages.
  • Performance AnalystReads the numbers, flags what is stuck and prepares small local ad ideas for approval.
  • Campaign BuilderDrafts weekly posts in your voice and plans the posting schedule.
  • Technical AuditorChecks your website for errors, unreadable pages and missing details.
  • Support TriageSorts incoming messages, drafts replies and flags unhappy customers.
  • Outbound StrategistFinds nearby businesses that need this and drafts the first message for review.

What we do not promise. Nobody controls what an AI says, and answers change from day to day. We promise careful measurement, steady work and honest reporting. Nothing is sent or published without a human approving it.
